Game Rules

How to Play Table Tennis?

Rule Description
🏓 Objective Hit the ball with your paddle to score points. Don't let it pass you on the left!
🖱️ Controls Move your mouse up/down over the canvas. Use Arrow Up / Arrow Down keys or W/S on keyboard.
🤖 AI Opponent The AI controls the right paddle and tracks the ball automatically. It can make mistakes!
🎯 Scoring You score when the ball passes the AI paddle on the right side. AI scores when it passes you.
🏆 Winning The first player to reach 11 points wins the match. Click Restart to play again.
⚡ Speed The ball gradually speeds up with each paddle hit, making the game more challenging!
